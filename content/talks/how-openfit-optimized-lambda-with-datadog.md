<p>Going serverless has many advantages, including simplified architecture, faster deployments, and reduced costs—especially on infrastructure—as Lambda pricing depends on the resources allocated to each function. This introduces a new challenge, however: how can you ensure that you are optimizing your Lambda function size whilst also providing the best possible experience for your customers?</p>
<p>In this talk, we’ll dive into Openfit’s serverless journey, and show you how they leveraged Datadog to monitor their functions and optimize their cloud bill.</p>
