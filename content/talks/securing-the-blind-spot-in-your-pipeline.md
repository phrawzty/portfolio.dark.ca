<p>Do you know what’s actually in your application? Modern apps rely on hundreds of third-party libraries, creating a massive, often silent security blind spot. Research confirms that package registries are rife with vulnerabilities—risks that remain hidden until they’re exploited. In this session, learn how AWS CodeArtifact and Datadog Code Security provide a unified defense. We’ll demonstrate how to use preventive controls to vet what enters your builds and detective controls to monitor production in real-time. Stop guessing and start owning your software supply chain with two essential layers of protection.</p>
