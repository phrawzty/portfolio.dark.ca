<p>The observability community is large and diverse, with no shortages of tools or strategies being recommended by various vendors in the space. Keeping up with how different teams are addressing their observability challenges and what’s effective or not can be daunting when trying to prepare for the road ahead. Join experts from Lightstep, Datadog, Honeycomb, and Grafana Labs for a panel discussion on the current state of the observability community, its future, and what practitioners should be aware of as they plan for next year and beyond.</p>
