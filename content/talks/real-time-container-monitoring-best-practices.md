<p>To accelerate delivery and scale of applications in today’s rapid-paced market, organisations have migrated their workloads to containers, using technologies like Amazon ECS, EKS, and Fargate.</p>
<p>As stacks scale and container orchestration becomes more complex, organisations can now take advantage of a wide range of tools, services, platforms, and technologies to manage their growing infrastructure.</p>
<p>In this session, technologists from Datadog and AWS will explore the top trends in containers and share how to obtain full-stack visibility into containerised environments.</p>
