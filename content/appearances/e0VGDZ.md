<p>This Datadog + Kong webinar will show a comprehensive observability solution covering an end-to-end Kubernetes deployment process, including a Canary Release traffic control and management model. We’ll cover:</p>
<ul>
<li>Rapid debugging, troubleshooting, and investigation</li>
<li>Jump starting exploration with Log Patterns</li>
<li>Generating metrics from microservice logs to track trends and KPIs</li>
</ul>
<p>Combining comprehensive observability capabilities with powerful platform and system management, attendees will learn how Datadog and Kong Mesh work together to deploy, manage, and evolve their mission-critical applications.</p>
