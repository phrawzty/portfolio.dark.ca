<p>The most prolific attacks of 2025 and 2026 (so far) have seen maintainers phished out of their accounts, worms spread through registries on their own, and malware hijack developers’ own AI coding agents. But underneath, they’re all the same attack: trusted identities abusing privileges faster than anyone can react. AI agents run through the whole story, because they’re the newest and least-governed identities most organizations have; however, the foundations of secrets, authentication, and pipeline hygiene only matter more in the agentic era, not less.</p>
<p>This talk turns that into three shifts every DevSecOps team needs to make: in how they think about identity, how they verify trust, and where they put their guardrails. Drawing on real, documented incidents and open-source, vendor-neutral tooling, we’ll look at how these attacks were designed, why they were successful, how they were detected, and what you need to do to protect yourself going forward. Whether you write the pipelines or manage the people who do, you’ll leave with a practical way to reason about supply-chain risk that will outlast the next clever malware wave.</p>
